Azurite is a bright blue or sometimes
a greenish-blue & is usually associated
with Malachite. Azurite was the most
important pigment in European painting
Throughout the middle ages and the
Renaissance. Azurite comes from a borrowed
Persian word "lazhward".
Occasional use of azurite as a paint
pigment began with the Egyptians.
Azurite was found in paint pigment as
early as the Fourth Dynasty in Egypt.
Azurite was often used in tempera
paintings of the Middle Ages when
ultramarine (lazurite) was not available
or considered too expensive. In oil painting
azurite was used since it was much
less expensive than ultramarine. It was
often used in underlayers of paintings
to strengthen the weak tinting strength
of ultramarine
